Akash Thakur

Akash Thakur

Cognizant

AI systems are rapidly evolving from assistive tools into autonomous decision-makers operating in production environments. While most organizations still evaluate AI success primarily through accuracy, latency, or model performance—often overlooking reliability, failure modes, and human trust.

In this talk, I will introduce AI Reliability Engineering (AIRE)—an emerging discipline that applies Site Reliability Engineering principles to AI-driven systems. I will explore how AI fails differently than traditional software, why AI-related incidents often go undetected, and how foundational SRE concepts such as SLOs, error budgets, observability, and graceful degradation must evolve to support reliable AI workloads at scale.

Akash Thakur is a Site Reliability Engineering leader and IT Architect with 17+ years of experience modernizing mission-critical systems across finance, healthcare, and the public sector. He writes and speaks on the evolving intersection of SRE and AI infrastructure

Nandakishor Koka

Nandakishor Koka

Disney

As organizations deploy AI agents to answer questions, automate decisions, and take action on enterprise data, semantic models have quietly become part of the AI execution layer. Metrics, dimensions, joins, and business definitions no longer power just dashboards—they increasingly ground large language models, retrieval systems, and autonomous agents. This shift raises a critical but underappreciated risk: AI agents inherit the operational weaknesses of the semantic models beneath them. Undocumented metric changes, unversioned definitions, and ungoverned joins do not merely produce incorrect dashboards—they propagate directly into AI reasoning, recommendations, and automated actions. This talk argues that DevOps discipline is a prerequisite for trustworthy AI agents. We examine how CI/CD, versioning, observability, and approval workflows - traditionally applied to application code—must extend to semantic models to prevent AI hallucinations, reasoning drift, and silent failures. Attendees will learn why treating semantics as production infrastructure is essential for scaling AI safely, and how DevOps practices form the missing link between analytics platforms and reliable agentic systems.

Nandakishor Koka is a Principal Technical Architect at The Walt Disney Company, specializing in engineering management, data platforms, and large-scale analytics solutions. With prior roles including Principal Data Scientist at Fractal Analytics and leadership positions at Mu Sigma and Infosys, he brings more than two decades of experience architecting enterprise data systems, building machine learning solutions, and leading cross-functional engineering teams.
